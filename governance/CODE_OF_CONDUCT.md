# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in the BOSC Community Library respectful, inclusive, and productive for learners, educators, contributors, maintainers, and public-sector partners.

## Expected Behavior

- Use welcoming and respectful language.
- Accept constructive feedback professionally.
- Focus discussion on the quality, accessibility, and public value of the library.
- Credit original authors and resource providers.
- Report inaccurate, inaccessible, or legally unclear resources.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Plagiarism or claiming ownership of work created by others.
- Submitting resources that violate copyright, privacy, or learner safety.
- Disrupting issues, pull requests, or reviews with unrelated arguments.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, reject pull requests, close issues, or restrict participation when behavior violates this code. Serious or repeated violations may result in a permanent ban from project spaces.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns by opening a private communication with the lead maintainer or by using the repository issue tracker if the concern is not sensitive. Reports should include the date, location, people involved, and a short description of what happened.

Maintainers will review reports fairly, keep sensitive information limited to those who need it, and respond with proportionate action.
