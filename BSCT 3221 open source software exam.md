**Overview & Scenario**

You are appointed as the **Lead Community Maintainer** for the "BOSC
Community Library". Because this is a take-home exam, you are expected
to demonstrate an **iterative development lifecycle**. You are not just
building a project; you are curating a professional Open Source
environment that follows global standards of transparency, legal
compliance, and community health.

**Phase 1: Advanced Repository Architecture (10%)**

**Competency:** Design thinking in developing scalable, secure wireless
communication systems (adapted to OSS structures).

1.  **Repository Setup:** Create a public GitHub repository titled
    BOSC-Community-Library.

2.  **Professional Structure:** Beyond basic folders, you must include:

    - .github/ directory containing an issue template and a pull request
      template.

    - CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md outlining community behavior standards.

    - CONTRIBUTING.md providing detailed technical instructions for
      external contributors.

3.  **Git History:** Your commit history must show consistent activity
    over the 7-day period. Single-day "bulk uploads" will be penalized.

**Phase 2: Legal Audit & Disciplinary Rationale (10%)**

**Competency:** Critical analysis of legal and ethical dimensions in
OSS.

1.  **License Implementation:** Apply a license .

2.  **Extended Legal Essay:** In a file named LEGAL_ANALYSIS.md, provide
    a **500-word analysis** covering:

    - Why your chosen license is superior for a public-sector project
      (e.g., government transparency).

    - How your license handles **patent grants** or **trademark
      protections** compared to others.

    - The implications for a commercial entity wanting to build a "paid
      version" of your library.

**Phase 3: The "Five-Issue" Mastery Challenge (15%)**

**Competency:** Skill in contributing to and documenting open source
participation.

You must identify and resolve **five (5) complex issues**. As this is a
take-home exam, simple typos do not count. Issues must involve:

1.  **Functional Bug (x2):** Identify and fix broken logic or missing
    links in the library's resource index.

2.  **Feature Enhancement (x2):** Implement a new system feature (e.g.,
    a localized language support section or a searchable resource
    database).

3.  **Refactoring/Maintenance (x1):** Improve the repository's
    performance or file organization.

- **Protocol:** Each issue must have an open discussion thread, a
  dedicated feature branch, and a merged Pull Request with a "Peer
  Review" comment (you may simulate the review or ask a peer for a
  comment).

**Phase 4: Sustainability & Public-Sector Strategy (5%)**

**Competency:** Evaluation of OSS economic models and communication of
benefits to diverse audiences.

1.  **Sustainability Model:** In SUSTAINABILITY.md, develop a detailed
    **funding strategy**. Compare the "Red Hat" service-based model
    against a "Foundation-driven" model for this library.

2.  **Government Pitch:** Write a formal proposal (within the
    repository) addressed to the Ministry of Education, advocating for
    the adoption of your library over proprietary alternatives, focusing
    on **Total Cost of Ownership (TCO)** and **vendor lock-in**.
