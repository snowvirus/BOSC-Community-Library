# BOSC Community Library - Luganda Summary

## Ebikwata ku Pulojekiti eno

BOSC Community Library kitabo kya ngeri eya digital mwe tusobola okufuna ebirabo n’ebintu ebisomesa ebiyamba abasomesa, abayizi n’omuntu owabulijjo mu kitundu. Pulojekiti eno yakolebwa okuyamba abantu okufuna amagezi n’ebikozesebwa mu kusoma awatali kusasula ssente nnyingi eri makampuni amanene ag’ebitabo.

## Emitendera Gy’ebisomesebwa

1. **Digital Literacy (Okumanya Ebikwata ku Kompyuta):** Tuyigiriza abantu engeri y’okukozesaamu kompyuta n’emitimbagano mu ngeri ey’obuvunaanyizibwa.
2. **Open Science (Ssayansi Atali wa Kyama):** Okukozesa ebisomesebwa mu ssayansi ebitaliko bisale.
3. **Teacher Training (Okutendeka Abasomesa):** Okuyamba abasomesa okufuna ebisomesebwa ebitayita mu makampuni amapanga.

## Engeri y’Okuyambamu

Buli muntu akkirizibwa okwongerako ku nteekateeka eno. Bw’oba olina ekisomesebwa ky’oyagala okugabana n’abalala, wandiika ku muko gwaffe ogwa GitHub.
